How Do I Know Which Treatment is Best For Me?

Determining the best treatment for your low sex drive involves a comprehensive evaluation by a Gameday healthcare provider. During your initial consultation, your provider will conduct a thorough health assessment, which includes a detailed discussion of your medical history, lifestyle, and any symptoms you’re experiencing.

They might also perform a physical examination and order blood tests to check your hormone levels, particularly testosterone. This comprehensive evaluation helps pinpoint the underlying causes of your low libido, whether it’s due to hormonal imbalances, stress, fatigue, medication side effects, or other health conditions.

Based on the findings, your Gameday healthcare provider will then recommend a personalized treatment plan tailored to your specific needs. The goal is to choose the most effective treatment that addresses the root cause of your low sex drive and aligns with your overall health and lifestyle.
