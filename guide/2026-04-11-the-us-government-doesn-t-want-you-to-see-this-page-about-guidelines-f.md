EXPERT OPINION: Dr. Nayna Shah, MD; Dr. Ashley Navarro, MD,Access to comprehensive sex education is an invaluable resource because it reduces stigma and shame around healthy sexual behaviors, equips young people with the knowledge they need to make informed decisions about their bodies, and empowers them to engage in meaningful conversations and healthy relationships. The connection between inadequate sex education and increased unintended pregnancy, sexually transmitted infection (STI) rates, and sexual violence in this country is well-known and well-studied. Providing young people will the necessary skills and resources to make informed and healthy decisions about sex and relationships is more effective than blocking important information and simply telling them to not engage in sexual behaviors. During this somber time of censorship and erasure of crucial sex education curricula, evidence-based information can still be found through Planned Parenthood, Bedsider, AHSA, and Scarleteen. Through the dissemination of accurate sexual health information, we can preserve reproductive justice and bodily autonomy and foster safer and healthier communities.—Dr. Nayna Shah, MD, is a Complex Family Planning Fellow at Boston Medical Center. Dr. Ashley Navarro, MD, MPH, is a Complex Family Planning Specialist/OBGYN Generalist at Boston Medical Center.
