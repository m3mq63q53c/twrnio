What is Arimistane?

Arimistane or Androsta-3 5-diene-7 17-dione (say that 5 times fast) is known as an Aromatase Inhibitor. This group of drugs is known for leaving the Aromatase enzyme inactive, thereby stopping the conversion of testosterone into estrogen.

Commonly referred to as Anti Estrogen compounds, these are often used to manage estrogen levels of athletes who make use of anabolic steroids, or those who use Testosterone Replacement Therapy.

While men do need a certain amount of estrogen in their bodies, having far too much could lead to certain side effects such as gyno development, water retention, and even body composition changes.

Low estrogen on the other hand also has side effects, such as limiting muscle mass gain, libido problems, and horrible cardiovascular health.

Finding a happy medium of estrogen levels in the body is key, but also incredibly hard to do. This is why you must use the right estrogen blockers to put you in the sweet zone,

How Does Armistane Work (Aromatase Inhibitor)?

How exactly does an Aromatase Inhibitor work? In the body, particularly in fat cells, there are enzymes called aromatase, an estrogen synthetase, or estrogen synthase.

Testosterone will interact with aromatase and convert it into oestradiol (estrogen). While there are detailed calculations to find out how much you may be converting, the easiest way to check is to have your blood work done.

Normally, some testosterone will convert, and you’d be fine. However, with the introduction of more hormones, you can expect more estrogen. This is why many bodybuilders and other athletes will make use of cycle support to ensure their levels remain relatively stable and normal.

Arimistane, funnily enough, is often referred to as a prohormone, because it has a similar structure to 7-Keto-DHEA.

An interesting fact about Arimstane is the fact that it is a suicide inhibitor. This means it will not only leave the aromatase enzyme inactive but also remove it. In doing so, you limit estrogen rebound. Aromasin is another popular suicide inhibitor, and these are often used in favor of Arimidex and Letrozole for that reason.

Arimistane will also help with natural testosterone levels, as it will act as a testosterone booster by increasing LH and FSH levels in the body.

Arimistane Dosage on Cycle

It is not uncommon to hear guys wanting to take something while on a cycle to avoid complete Testosterone shutdown. Sometimes called “LH Support”, as keeping LH levels high will keep endogenous testosterone levels high.
