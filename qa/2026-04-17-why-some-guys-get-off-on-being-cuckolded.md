More specifically, it was assumed that a “cuck” could not sexually satisfy his partner, which then forced her to get her needs met by someone else.

“The verb cuckolding [has been] used to describe the patriarchal fear among men that they would be humiliated and lose social status should their wife cheat on them, become pregnant, and they would raise the other man's child none-the-wiser,” says McDevitt.
