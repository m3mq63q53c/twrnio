At 37 weeks pregnant, your body is getting ready for labor, which may cause some changes and discomfort in the vaginal area. Studies have shown that many pregnant women experience pelvic pain as the ligaments and tissues stretch to make room for your growing baby. This kind of pain can be normal, like mild pressure or soreness. However, there are times when vaginal pain may need prompt attention. You should contact your healthcare provider if:

The pain is very strong or gets worse quickly.

You notice heavy bleeding or watery fluids.

You have a fever, chills, or feel very unwell.
