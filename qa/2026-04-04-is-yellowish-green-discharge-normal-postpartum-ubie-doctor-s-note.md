Is yellowish-green discharge normal postpartum?

Internal Medicine

A yellowish-green discharge after giving birth is not usually normal and may signal an infection. It is important to talk to a doctor if you notice this color or have other symptoms.

Explanation

After a baby is born, women normally have a discharge called lochia. Lochia usually starts off red and then changes to a pinkish or brown color before eventually turning whitish. A yellowish-green color is not what we expect as part of this normal process. That color might mean that there is an infection inside the uterus or another problem that needs attention. Some women might have a small amount of yellowish discharge soon after birth, but if it becomes persistent or is associated with a strong smell, pain, fever, or feeling unwell, these could be signs of an infection. Infections need treatment with the right medicine, so it is very important to speak with a healthcare provider if you notice these changes. Your doctor or nurse can check you carefully to see if there is an infection or another issue that needs treatment. Keeping track of changes and discussing any concerns during postpartum care, as recommended by maternal health guides and services, is an important way to maintain your health after giving birth.
