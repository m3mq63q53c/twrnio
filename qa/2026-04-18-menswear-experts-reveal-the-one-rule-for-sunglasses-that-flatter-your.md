The beauty of an oval shape is that most shades will look great on you. That said, the trick is selecting sunglasses that aren’t wider than your face. Your best best is to try a slightly more oversized, rounded style that isn’t too massive where you end up looking like some sort of bug. That’s not a good look on anyone.

Best Sunglasses for Oblong Faces

It’s all about balance for a face that’s longer in nature than the rest of the shapes. Meaning: Ignore any sunglasses that are too small or too angular. These will end up appearing out of place. Here, you’ll win most with specs that are more square in design to counteract that long silhouette.

Best Sunglasses for Square Faces

If you have a stronger face shape with a square jaw, go for sunglasses with softer lines and rounded corners. This style will soften your features and compliment a square jaw and a shape where your jawline, cheekbones, and forehead are fairly even.

Best Sunglasses for Round Faces

While there is certainly nothing wrong with a round silhouette, avoiding anything too circular will offset some of that roundness. If you choose a round pair of sunglasses that mimics your face shape, they will actually accentuate your rounder cheeks more. So more angular or square frames will work best here for a flattering vibe.
