There are a few reasons why you might have pain in your left thigh. These are a few possibilities, although there are many other additional causes.

Meralgia Paresthetica: This is a condition where a nerve in your thigh gets pinched. It can cause pain, tingling, or numbness on the outer part of your thigh.

Greater Trochanter Bursitis: This is when a small sac near your hip joint gets irritated. It can cause pain on the outside of your thigh and hip.
