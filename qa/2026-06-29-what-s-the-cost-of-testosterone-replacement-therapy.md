What is testosterone replacement therapy, and what does it treat?

Testosterone is a male steroid hormone most commonly known to be linked to a healthy sex drive. This hormone also affects body fat, bone density, muscle mass, mood, and red blood cell count. As men age, testosterone production levels can begin to decline. Testosterone Replacement Therapy (TRT) is a treatment that supplements testosterone to the body to bring levels within a normal range.
