Does Tongkat Ali Actually Boost Sex Drive? The Science and Next Steps

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Tongkat Ali may boost sex drive in some men, particularly those with low testosterone or stress-related hormonal suppression. Small clinical studies suggest modest improvements in testosterone levels, libido, energy, and mood — though results vary and the overall evidence remains limited.

Several factors influence whether Tongkat Ali is right for you, including underlying medical causes of low libido, safety precautions, drug interactions, and appropriate dosing. Some men benefit significantly, while others may need lab testing, hormone evaluation, or medical treatment instead of a supplement.

Because low sex drive can stem from many causes — including low testosterone, thyroid issues, depression, medication side effects, or chronic stress — it's important to understand what's actually driving your symptoms before choosing a supplement. For most healthy adults, short-term use appears reasonably safe.

It may not be safe for people with hormone-sensitive conditions

It may interact with medications affecting hormones

Long-term safety data is limited

People with heart disease, prostate conditions, or serious medical illness should speak with a doctor first
