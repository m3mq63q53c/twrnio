About the Symptom

Is the presence of foreign material (organic or inorganic) in the nasal cavity. These foreign objects should only be removed by medical professionals as they may become airway foreign bodies if pushed too far toward the back of the nose. If left in place, they can cause infection, swelling, pain, or drainage from the side of the foreign object. Some may be medical emergencies including button batteries that can cause major damage to the structures of the nose.
