Explanation

Fertility issues are quite common and affect many couples around the world. According to the medical literature, about 10-15% of couples face challenges when trying to conceive. Infertility is defined as not being able to get pregnant after a year of trying for women under 35, or after six months for women 35 and older.

Causes of Infertility:

Female Factors: Issues like problems with ovulation, blocked fallopian tubes, or conditions like endometriosis.

Diagnosis and Management:

Doctors use different tests to find out why a couple is having trouble getting pregnant. This can include blood tests, imaging tests, or checking the health of the sperm.

Treatments might involve lifestyle changes, medications, or procedures like in vitro fertilization (IVF).
