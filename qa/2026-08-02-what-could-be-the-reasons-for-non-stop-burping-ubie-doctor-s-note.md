Explanation

Non-stop burping, also known as excessive belching, can be caused by a few different things. One common reason is aerophagia, which means swallowing too much air. This can happen if you eat or drink too fast, chew gum, or drink fizzy drinks.

Sometimes, burping happens because of issues in the digestive system. For example, if the stomach is not working well, gas in the gastric content then comes out as burps.

There are also conditions like gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD) that can cause more burping. In GERD, acid from the stomach comes back up into the esophagus, which coluld make you burp more.

If someone is burping a lot and it bothers them, it might be helpful to talk to a doctor. They can help figure out the cause and suggest what to do next.
