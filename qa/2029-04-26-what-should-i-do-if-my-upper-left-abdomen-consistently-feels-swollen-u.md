Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS): This is a common condition that affects the digestive system. People with IBS might experience bloating, abdominal pain, and changes in bowel habits. Managing IBS often involves dietary changes, stress management, and sometimes medication.

Sugar Malabsorption: Some people have trouble digesting certain sugars, which can cause bloating and discomfort. This is because the sugars aren't broken down properly, leading to gas and swelling.
