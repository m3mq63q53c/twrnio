For many years, doctors have treated erectile dysfunction (ED) with medicines called PDE5 inhibitors, like sildenafil. Recently, researchers have been working on new drugs that improve on these treatments by helping the blood flow and relaxing muscles in the penis even better. These new medicines focus on different pathways in the body and might have fewer side effects than older options. In addition to medications, other treatments are also being studied. One exciting area is low-intensity shockwave therapy. This treatment uses gentle sound waves to improve blood flow and help repair small blood vessel damage in the penis. Scientists are also exploring regenerative treatments, such as stem cell and gene therapies, which aim to restore normal function from within. While these new treatments show promise, many are still in the research phase and not yet widely available. If you or someone you know is dealing with ED, it is important to talk with your healthcare provider about the options that are best for you.
