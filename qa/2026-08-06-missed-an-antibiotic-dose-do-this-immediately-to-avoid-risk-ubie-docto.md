Missing an antibiotic dose can impact the effectiveness of the treatment. When you skip a dose, the level of medicine in your body may drop, which can give bacteria a chance to grow and become stronger. This can make it harder to get better and may lead to antibiotic resistance, where bacteria become harder to kill with medicines.

What to Do if You Miss a Dose:

Take it as soon as you remember: If it's close to the time for your next dose, skip the missed one and continue with your regular schedule.

Do not double up: Avoid taking two doses at the same time to make up for a missed dose.

Why It Matters:

Consistency is Key: Taking antibiotics exactly as prescribed helps ensure that the medicine can work effectively to fight the infection.
