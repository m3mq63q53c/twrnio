Explanation

During pregnancy, many women experience various aches and discomfort because their bodies are changing. However, upper left abdominal pain is not something that is generally considered normal. While there can be mild abdominal discomfort due to normal digestive changes or the growing uterus pressing on nearby organs, persistent or severe pain in the upper left region should be taken seriously.

Some conditions that might cause pain in that area include gastrointestinal issues, such as gastritis, or even gallstones. In pregnancy, the body undergoes many hormonal and structural changes that sometimes bring on unusual symptoms.
