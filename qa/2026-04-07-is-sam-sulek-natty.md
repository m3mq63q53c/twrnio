What Does “Natty” Mean in Bodybuilding?

In the bodybuilding community, the word “natty” is short for natural. A “natty” lifter is someone who builds their physique without the use of performance-enhancing drugs (PEDs) such as anabolic steroids, human growth hormone (HGH), or selective androgen receptor modulators (SARMs). At its core, being natty means relying on training, diet, rest, and legal supplements like protein powder or creatine.

Natty vs. Enhanced Bodybuilding

The term has become a major talking point because it draws a line between natural bodybuilding and enhanced bodybuilding. Natural bodybuilding federations—such as the International Natural Bodybuilding Association (INBA) or the World Natural Bodybuilding Federation (WNBF)—require strict drug testing. By contrast, enhanced competitions like Mr. Olympia are widely acknowledged to feature athletes who use PEDs.
