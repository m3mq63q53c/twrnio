Explanation

Legionnaires' disease is caused by bacteria that can grow in water systems. To prevent it at home, it's important to keep water systems clean. This includes regularly cleaning hot tubs, showers, and faucets. Here are some steps to help prevent Legionnaires' disease:

Regular Cleaning: Make sure to clean and disinfect water systems like hot tubs and showers.

Water Temperature: Keep hot water heaters set to a temperature that is too hot for bacteria to grow, but be careful to avoid scalding.
