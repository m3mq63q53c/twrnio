# Fox News Host Neil Cavuto Tells Viewers 'Stop the Suffering' and Get Vaccinated

Neil Cavuto, an anchor with Fox News, has publicly urged viewers to get vaccinated in order to stem the spread of the Covid pandemic. Cavuto went on MediaBuzz to speak about the importance of vaccines, and shared his own experience of getting Covid.

Cavuto is immunocompromised, having undergone treatment for stage 4 Hodgkin's lymphoma, which is now in remission, as well as living with multiple sclerosis. His health issues prompted him to get vaccinated, which meant that when he contracted Covid his prognosis was better than it could have been: doctors reportedly informed him that he would be in a "far more dire situation" had he been unvaccinated.

"Life is too short to be an ass," he said. "Life is way too short to be ignorant of the promise of something that is helping people worldwide. Stop the deaths. Stop the suffering. Please get vaccinated. Please... I'm surviving this because I did."

"Take the political speaking points and toss them for now," he said. "In the end, if you can get vaccinated and think of someone else and think of what that could mean to them and their survivability from something like this, we'll all be better off."
