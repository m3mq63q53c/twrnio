What are the early signs of thinning hair?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Early signs of thinning hair include increased hair shedding, a more visible scalp, and a widening part. You may also notice a smaller ponytail, more hair on your pillow or in the shower drain, and a receding or higher hairline. Changes in hair texture, such as finer or weaker strands, can also signal early thinning.
