# Pelicans Forward Zion Williamson Looks Jacked After Quarantine

The New Orleans Pelicans posted a photo of the team's newly 20-year old forward Zion Williamson working out and looking more jacked than ever on Instagram. As often happens with posts related to the basketball phenom (never forget the shoe blowout that cratered Nike's reputation for a brief moment), the photo spurred a comment storm from all over social media.

"Built for this 💪," the team captioned the post.

The post featured other Pelicans players like Jrue Holiday, JJ Reddick, Josh Hart and Nicolò Melli, but Williamson's physique stole the comment section.

"Damn Zion looks to be in better shape 🔥," one fan commented, while others wrote, "Zion looking lean😈🔥", "Zion looking like Hulk lol 💪🏾", and "Zion looks cut".

And on Twitter, the reactions were much the same.

The Pelicans are gearing up for the restart of the postponed NBA season. They're one of 22 teams set to play when the season re-starts later this month. Last Thursday afternoon, ESPN reported that the NBA Board of Governors voted to give the league the chance to form into 22 teams that will meet in Orlando, Florida, for a continuation of the 2019-2020 season that stopped abruptly because of the pandemic. The Pelicans are currently scheduled to play in the first nationally televised game for the NBA's restart against the Utah Jazz on July 30, just shy of four months after the league's last game on March 11.

And during the team's time off, Williamson has clearly been in the gym, as documented by the Pelicans social media.
