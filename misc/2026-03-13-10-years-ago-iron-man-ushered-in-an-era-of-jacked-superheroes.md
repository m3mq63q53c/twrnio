# 10 Years Ago, 'Iron Man' Ushered In an Era Of Jacked Superheroes

Ten years ago, director Jon Favreau debuted Iron Man, starring a troubled Robert Downey Jr. in the middle of a comeback tour playing one of Marvel's more obscure characters, mostly notable for getting called "shellhead" throughout the 1960s.

Somehow, the film crushed the box office, jumpstarting an unprecedented streak of 18 shared-universe movies that took home roughly a bajillion dollars altogether — a formula that studios have been trying (and failing, hard) to replicate with everything from King Arthur to the Creature from the Black Lagoon.
