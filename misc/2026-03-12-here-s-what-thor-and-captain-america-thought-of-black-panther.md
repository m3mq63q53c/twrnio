# Here's What Thor and Captain America Thought of 'Black Panther'

Let's face it: As much as we love Chris Pratt, Chris Hemsworth, Chris Pine, and Chris Evans, it can be hard to tell the Hollywood actors apart. Besides having the same first name, they all star in our favorite superhero and sci-fi movies; their fitness games are all on-point; if you blurred your eyes and looked at the four of them, you'd be hard-pressed to tell which Chris was which.

To help you keep track of everyone's favorite Chrises, we're rounding up what each of them got up to during the week. Welcome to This Week in Chrises.

Chris Pratt: Loved Black Panther just as much as the rest of us

Fellow Marvel guy Chris Pratt echoed all of America this week when he took to Twitter to lose his mind over the new Black Panther movie.

"THE HYPE IS REAL!! Woke up thinking about #BlackPanther It’s like a Bond movie meets Shakespearean tragedy," Pratt wrote. "Great action. Stunning visuals. Exceptional performances all around. Thought provoking AND Funny!" He even signed it with a clapping emoji, as one does to truly show their love for a film.

Chris Evans: Teased another new Marvel movie

The Marvel Chrises were truly out in full force this week. While Chris Evans shared his love for the Black Panther movie as well, he also might have confirmed a forthcoming new Marvel movie.

While speaking to ET Canada, Evans said, “There’s nothing Marvel can’t do, and I’m sure it’s gonna have the exact same effect when Captain Marvel comes out, and then the Black Widow movie comes out. Marvel just has the winning recipe and they’re kind of one step ahead of everybody else.”

Did he just confirm a solo Black Widow movie is coming? Because it sure as hell sounds like it.

Chris Pine: Took the week off

Nothing new from Chris Pine this week! The guy has no official social media and only carries a flip-phone. Maybe he drove around in his Porsche 356 convertible Speedster. Or maybe he saw Black Panther. And liked it.
