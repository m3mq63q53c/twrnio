Explanation

Period cramps, also known as dysmenorrhea, can be very painful for many people. Naproxen is effective in treating period cramps. It works by reducing the levels of prostaglandins, which are chemicals in the body that cause the uterus to contract and lead to pain during menstruation. Ibuprofen is another treatment that works similarly to treat period cramps. Another over-the-counter option is acetaminophen or paracetamol, which can also help relieve pain.

How Naproxen and Ibuprofen Work:

[Period Pain Now](https://47466.com/lvc5q8/)

Naproxen and ibuprofen are two types of nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs).

They help lower the production of prostaglandins, which are higher during menstruation and cause cramps.

Overall, naproxen, ibuprofen, acetaminophen and paracetamol are well-studied and commonly used options for managing period cramps, making it easier for those affected to go about their daily activities with less discomfort.
