Common Causes of Dry Skin

Dry skin can be triggered or worsened by several lifestyle and environmental factors, including:

Cold or windy weather (especially New Hampshire winters)

Long, hot showers or over-cleansing

Age-related loss of natural oils

Harsh or alcohol-based skincare products

Exposure to sun, pollution, or indoor heating

No matter the cause, the right hydrating facial can help restore comfort, moisture, and skin resilience.
