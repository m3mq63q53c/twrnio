What is Adhesive Capsulitis?

Also known as "frozen shoulder". This condition is caused by inflammation of the ligaments and capsule around the shoulder joint. As the disease progresses, patients find it increasingly difficult to move the shoulder joint. Risk factors include diabetes, thyroid conditions, age, and injury to the joint.

Typical Symptoms of Adhesive Capsulitis

Moving the arm / shoulder worsens the pain

Pain in the upper arms (from shoulder down to elbow)

Pain when exerting physically e.g. climbing the stairs

Arm pain

Shoulder pain more severe when lifting arm by itself than when supported by other hand

Pain changes with posture

Shoulder pain

Joint pain, swelling, or discomfort

Diagnostic Questions for Adhesive Capsulitis

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Does moving your arms or shoulders make your pain worse?

Do your upper arms hurt?

Does pain improve with rest but worsen with movement?

Are you experiencing arm pain?

Is your shoulder pain worse when lifting your arm alone compared to when supporting it with your other hand?

Treatment of Adhesive Capsulitis

Frozen shoulder can takes 6-18 months for full recovery. Anti inflammatory medications and gentle stretching and physiotherapy can help maintain shoulder flexibility and strength during this time. When severe, interventions such as corticosteroid medications, injections or surgical interventions may be necessary to relax or release the thickened and inflamed capsule of the shoulder
