How does menopause affect frequent urination at night?

[Now Menopause](https://hostdefense-journal.escortskhi.com/)

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Menopause often causes frequent nighttime urination, a condition called nocturia. Declining estrogen levels during menopause weaken the bladder lining, reduce bladder capacity, and disrupt the body's fluid regulation, leading to more trips to the bathroom at night and disrupted sleep.

If you're experiencing nocturia or other menopause-related symptoms, understanding the root cause is the first step toward relief. Because symptoms can overlap with other conditions like urinary tract infections, overactive bladder, or diabetes, getting clarity matters. During menopause, many women experience changes in their bodies due to shifts in hormone levels. One common issue is nocturia, which is the need to urinate frequently during the night.

Hormonal Changes

The drop in estrogen levels during menopause affects the bladder and the tissues around it. This can lead to an increased need to urinate.
