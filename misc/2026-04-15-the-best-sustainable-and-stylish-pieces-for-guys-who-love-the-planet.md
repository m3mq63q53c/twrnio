# The Best Sustainable (and Stylish) Pieces for Guys Who Love the Planet

It behooves the planet and, well, the future of humanity, to invest in products that are sustainable and eco-minded. Luckily, the health of the environment has been top of mind lately and many designers and fashion companies have been doing their part to create stylish products that are agents of positive change. Here’s a list of some winter-ready goods from some of our favorite brands that happen to also be paving the way for a safer, healthier tomorrow.

Dockers® Tech Cargo

These stylish, tapered cargos—complete with hidden zipper pockets and 4-way stretch—are a wardrobe must-have that’ll easily take you from winter to spring, pairing just as nicely with boots and a sweater as with sneakers and a T-shirt. They’re also made using Water<Less® technology, meaning you’re helping to conserve one of Earth’s most precious resources. Feel good, look good, amirite?

Cariuma Oca Low-Top Sneaker

Brazilian footwear brand Cariuma has sustainability baked into its business plan—for each pair purchased, they plant two trees in the rainforest. And, yes, these skate-inspired shoes are aesthetically on point, but even better? They’re made by hand in ethical factories from natural materials and sent out in recyclable packaging.

Jungmaven Tie-Dye Hemp Socks

For Jungmaven, hemp is more than some sister plant of marijuana: it’s naturally clean (since its cultivation requires no irrigation, synthetic fertilizers, pesticides), antibacterial, and versatile. These tie-dye hemp socks boast all those advantages and have just the right amount of groovy, bohemian optimism to give your outfit some extra oomph.

Pangaia Recycled Cashmere Scarf

Recycled materials help minimize waste and give unused textiles a new life.
