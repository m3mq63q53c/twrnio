Explanation

Modern diets often include a lot of processed foods and sugary drinks, which can lead to obesity. These foods are usually high in calories but low in nutrients, making it easy to eat too much without getting the vitamins and minerals your body needs.

Key Factors Contributing to Obesity:

Processed Foods: These are foods that have been changed from their original form and often have added sugar, salt, and fat. They are easy to overeat because they taste good and don't make you feel full.

Sugary Drinks: Drinks like soda and sweetened juices have lots of sugar and calories but don't make you feel full, so you might drink them and still eat a lot.

Fast Food: Fast food is convenient but often high in unhealthy fats and calories, which can lead to weight gain.

These aspects of the modern diet can make it easy to eat too many calories. When we eat more calories than we use, our bodies store the extra energy as fat, leading to obesity.
