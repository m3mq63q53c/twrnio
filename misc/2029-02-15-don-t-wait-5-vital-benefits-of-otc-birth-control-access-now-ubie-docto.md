Over-the-counter birth control access offers several benefits that can improve the convenience and availability of contraceptives.

Easier Access: Allowing birth control pills to be sold over the counter means people can get them without needing a prescription from a doctor. This can be especially helpful for those who have trouble getting to a doctor or live in areas with few healthcare services.

Empowerment and Control: Having easier access to birth control lets people have more control over their reproductive health. It can help them decide when they want to have children by giving them a convenient way to prevent pregnancy.

Increased Use: When birth control is easier to get, more people might use it. This can lead to better family planning and fewer unplanned pregnancies.

In summary, making birth control available over the counter can help people manage their reproductive health more easily by removing some barriers to access.
