Internal Medicine

Yes, new treatments for narcolepsy type 2 and idiopathic hypersomnia are actively being developed. Emerging therapies focus on promoting wakefulness, reducing excessive daytime sleepiness, and better managing the broader symptoms that impact daily life. Research includes orexin receptor agonists, novel stimulants, and refined GABA-targeting medications, offering renewed hope for people living with these chronic sleep disorders.
