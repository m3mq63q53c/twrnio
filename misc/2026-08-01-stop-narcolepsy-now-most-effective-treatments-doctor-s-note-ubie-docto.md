Top medications for narcolepsy:

Modafinil (Provigil): A first-line stimulant that promotes daytime wakefulness.

Sodium oxybate (Xyrem): Improves nighttime sleep and reduces cataplexy episodes.

Key lifestyle changes:

Scheduled short naps (15–20 minutes)

Consistent sleep and wake times

Avoiding alcohol, caffeine, and heavy meals before bed

Regular exercise

Explanation

[Narcolepsy Now](https://glp-insights.xianfengchem.com/)

Narcolepsy is a sleep disorder that makes people very sleepy during the day. To help with this, doctors often use medicines like modafinil and sodium oxybate. These medicines help people stay awake and manage other symptoms like cataplexy, which is sudden muscle weakness.

Key Treatments:

Medications:

Modafinil: Helps keep you awake during the day.

Sodium Oxybate: Helps with nighttime sleep and reduces daytime sleepiness and cataplexy.

Other medicines can also be used depending on what symptoms need the most help.

Lifestyle Changes:

[Doctor Note Ubie](https://thymicshield.dns0663.com/blog/2639015045.html)

Scheduled Naps: Taking short naps during the day can help manage sleepiness.

Regular Sleep Schedule: Going to bed and waking up at the same time every day can help.
