What cooking practices can prevent salmonella contamination from chicken?

Salmonella infection from chicken is largely preventable with safe food handling. Cook chicken to an internal temperature of 165°F (74°C) to kill bacteria, and prevent cross-contamination by using separate cutting boards, utensils, and plates for raw poultry. Wash hands, surfaces, and tools thoroughly with hot, soapy water after contact with raw chicken. Refrigerate poultry promptly and avoid rinsing it before cooking, as this can spread bacteria. If you've recently eaten chicken and are experiencing symptoms like diarrhea, fever, stomach cramps, or nausea, you may be dealing with Salmonella or another foodborne illness — and identifying the cause early matters.
