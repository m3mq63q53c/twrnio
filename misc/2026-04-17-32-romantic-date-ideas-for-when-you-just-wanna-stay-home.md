# 32 Romantic Date Ideas for When You Just Wanna Stay Home

A great date might involve exploring the great outdoors, dining out at a restaurant, or visiting a museum, gallery, or other cultural site. But it can also take place within the comfort of your very own home. That's right: the at-home date night is an under-appreciated romantic activity. You get to see each other in comfy clothes, which can arguably be sexier than "nice" clothes. And if things get hot and heavy, well, the bedroom's right there. Plus, in the era of COVID-19—when many restaurants, bars, and other indoor gathering places are closed—the at-home date night can be a great way for committed couples to spice things up in a relationship gone stale from too many months spent in lockdown together.

Two important notes about the at-home date night. First, during the pandemic, it could be a high-risk move to bring a new partner back to your residence—you might be better off doing one of these quarantine date ideas instead. Second—pandemic or not—the at-home date could be perceived as a liiiittle forward for a first date. There's a lot of pressure when your bed's in the next room, and you don't want to rush into sexytime if you're not ready. If it's your first time meeting this person, you might want to try one of these first date ideas instead.

With that out of the way, let's get to the 32 best at-home date night ideas.
