Viral load refers to the volume of virus cells in the body. In general, the higher the viral load, the more likely you are to develop severe symptoms. During recovery, the decline of viral load improves the chances of getting better.

Peptides may help inhibit virus replication, making it more difficult for Covid-19 cells to pierce host cells, which is necessary to make more virus cells.
