Explanation

Moods are feelings that can last for a while and can change how we act. Some common types of mood affect include happiness, sadness, anger, and anxiety. Each mood can make us behave differently. For example, when we feel happy, we might smile more and be friendly. When we feel sad, we might not want to do things we usually like.

Types of Mood Affect:

Happiness: Often leads to positive behavior, like being more social and active.

Sadness: May cause withdrawal from activities and people.

Anger: Can result in aggressive or defensive actions.

Anxiety: Might lead to avoidance of certain situations or people.
