You know I already think two great tastes often taste great together – especially when it comes to style. The new collaboration between Opening Ceremony and Levi’s is the latest example in a string of smart pairings. This is the third capsule collection between the downtown boutique and the American denim icon. The Levi’s + Opening Ceremony collection has four pieces for men (and four for women), all rendered in premium, Japanese chambray and lightweight indigo denim. I especially like this killer denim pullover jacket below. It’s a perfect mix of Levi’s blue-collar roots and Opening Ceremony’s ultra-cool, modern aesthetic.

Although the inspiration is blue collar, the prices aren’t exactly lowbrow: they range from $175 - $300. You can get them at Levi’s and Opening Ceremony stores. For those of you who don’t live by either, don’t fret: you can pick up the goods at and Levi.com and OpeningCeremony.com.
