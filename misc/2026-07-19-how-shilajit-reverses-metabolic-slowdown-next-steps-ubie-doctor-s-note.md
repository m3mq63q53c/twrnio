1. Choose a High-Quality Source

Look for "Purified Shilajit" standardized to at least 50% fulvic acid.

Ensure third-party testing for heavy metals, pesticides, and microbes.

Avoid powders or capsules with fillers—aim for pure resin or standardized extract.

2. Determine the Right Dosage

Common dosing ranges from 300 mg to 500 mg per day of standardized extract.

Start low (100–200 mg daily) and gradually increase over 2–3 weeks.

Take with meals to improve absorption and reduce GI upset.

3. Monitor Effects and Side Effects

Track energy levels, fasting glucose, and any digestive symptoms.

Mild side effects can include stomach upset or headache—usually resolve with dose adjustment.

If you experience significant adverse reactions, discontinue and consult your doctor.
