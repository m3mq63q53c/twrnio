Brooklyn-based DJ and style influencer Mick Batyske is a rare breed.

In a profession notorious for late nights and excess, the man who routinely lends his talents to high-street and edgy fashion brands ranging from Ralph Lauren to Public School charges his batteries in the gym.

Beating battle ropes into the ground at luxe NYC gym S10 by day, and dropping beats for artists like Jay Z and Kanye West by night, Batyske is exactly who we would have expected to create this kind of playlist.

In a unique fitness-meets-fashion collaboration, artists were chosen by S10’s hive of high-profile style influencer clients, including Public School designer Dao-Yi Chow.

From there, Batyske took the reins, picking the tracks and mixing everything together.
