Explanation

After a stroke, muscle weakness occurs because the brain, which controls the muscles, is damaged. This damage makes it hard for the brain to send signals to the muscles, causing them to become weak. Over time, the muscles can also shrink or lose strength because they are not used as much.

To help make the muscles stronger again, rehabilitation is important. This can include different exercises and therapies:

[Stop Muscle Weakness](https://focus-and-calm.sunsimiaosd.com/blog/0265565069.html)

Physical Therapy: Helps improve movement and strength with special exercises.

Strength Training: Focuses on making muscles stronger through specific exercises.

[Stop Muscle](https://beautybiometrics.hbsnzj.com/)

Routine Assessments: Regular checks can help measure how strong the muscles are getting.

[Rehab Recovery](https://allpepay.com/blog/0565189873.html)

These methods can help the muscles get stronger and work better over time.
