Why "Egg Burps" Happen on Wegovy

Delayed Gastric EmptyingWegovy slows down how quickly your stomach empties food into your intestines. Food sits longer, ferments, and can release foul-smelling gases.

Low-Level Acid RefluxSmall amounts of stomach acid may flow back into the esophagus, carrying a sulfurous or eggy odor.

Dietary TriggersFoods high in sulfur compounds (eggs, cruciferous veggies, garlic, onions) can worsen odorous burps.

Instantly Effective Home Remedies

1. Sip Warm Ginger Tea

Why it helps: Ginger soothes the digestive tract, speeds up gastric emptying, and fights mild inflammation.

How to use: Steep fresh ginger slices in hot water for 5–10 minutes. Sip slowly when you feel a burp coming on.

2. Chew a Piece of Fennel or Cardamom

Why it helps: Fennel and cardamom seeds neutralize stomach acids and refresh breath.

How to use: Chew 2–3 seeds after meals or as soon as you sense egg burps.

3. Try Baking Soda and Water

Why it helps: Baking soda (sodium bicarbonate) quickly neutralizes excess stomach acid.

How to use: Stir ½ teaspoon of baking soda into 4–6 ounces of water. Drink slowly.

Caution: Don't use regularly if you're on a low-sodium diet or have high blood pressure.

4. Sip Apple Cider Vinegar (ACV)

Why it helps: Although acidic, ACV may balance stomach pH and improve digestion.

How to use: Mix 1 teaspoon of raw, unfiltered ACV into 8 ounces of water. Drink before or after meals.

Tip: If the taste is too strong, add a dash of honey.

5. Practice Diaphragmatic (Belly) Breathing

Why it helps: Deep, controlled breaths can reduce spasms of the lower esophageal sphincter, lowering reflux.

Sit upright with shoulders relaxed.

Inhale slowly through your nose, expanding your belly.

Exhale gently through your mouth.

Repeat for 1–2 minutes when you feel burps coming.

Lifestyle Tweaks for Long-Term Relief

Eat Smaller, More Frequent Meals

Large meals stretch the stomach, increasing pressure and reflux. Five to six lighter meals keep things moving.

Chew Food Thoroughly

Breaking food into smaller pieces helps it pass more quickly into the intestines, reducing fermentation.

Stay Upright After Eating

Avoid lying down or slouching for at least 2 hours post-meal. Gravity helps keep stomach contents down.

Avoid Carbonated Beverages

Fizzy drinks introduce extra gas, making burps more likely and odor more noticeable.

Limit Known Trigger Foods

Eggs (or pair with low-sulfur foods)

Cruciferous vegetables (broccoli, cauliflower, cabbage)

Garlic and onions

Red meats and processed meats

High-fat or fried foods

Wear Loose-Fitting Clothing

Tight waistbands can squeeze your stomach, pushing acid upward.

Maintain a Healthy Weight

Excess abdominal fat increases pressure on the stomach. Wegovy often helps with weight loss, which may ease reflux over time.

Elevate Your Head at Night

If nighttime burps or heartburn bother you, prop your upper body up 6–8 inches with pillows or a wedge. This prevents stomach acid from creeping upward.
