I'LL DIE ON the hill that it's better to be cold than hot, but I'm absolutely jonesing for summer. This past winter has been bleak and, as a native Texan, I'm not acclimated to weeks of freezing temps. I don't want to layer up in winter coats—even if they're suited to my morning commute—and I'm tired of clonking around in heavy boots and having dried out skin constantly. Really, I just want to wear a pair of shorts again. The thought of bearing thigh is what's helping me bear this extended cold.

Part of this coping mechanism is building a new shorts rotation because scoping out new pairs mentally warms my denim-clad legs (you should try it). I've been a Huckberry fan for years, and it's a frequent stop for my online window shopping. It's a good mix of greatest-hits and IYKYK brands. As I stacked my cart with chino shorts, trunks, linen and fatigues, I realized all my picks were from Huckberry's in-house labels. If you haven't checked them out lately—trust me—there's a lot, and they're good.
