# 11 Iconic Hat Brands Every Guy Should Know

Our product picks are editor-tested, expert-approved. We may earn a commission through links on our site. Why Trust Us?

The saying goes that hats aren't for everyone. We're here to tell you that we disagree.

Not only are they ideal for practical uses: wearing one in winter to keep you warm or donning one in summer to help protect you from the sun, they also serve as a way to amp up your style game. Recently, hats have re-entered every echelon of fashion, all the way from runways to simply just helping cover up our months-old haircuts during quarantine—and the trend is sticking.

While most of us have been sporting caps to the gym or on the beach for years now, it’s important to remember that the accessory goes beyond just your standard baseball style. Think about the more iconic fashion-forward options such as Indiana Jones’ fedora, Ice Cube’s cap from Boyz in The Hood, Charlie Chaplin’s bowler, or even Sherlock Holmes’ double-flapped topper: they all packed a large punch of style, while still feeling masculine and fashion-forward.

Below, we round up the top 11 hat brands that have cemented the art of hat making for the modern day man and highlight the different hat styles we suggest you incorporate into your closet.

Stetson

As far as American hats go, it's difficult to beat a Stetson. They use the highest quality materials and fuse them into a rugged Western aesthetic that feels both polished and masculine. This Bozeman Outdoor hat provides both ample shade and style.

Nick Fouquet

Fouquet represents the next generation of milliners. Their spin on classic hat shapes is undercut by quirky details, such as these embroidered dots on this straw fedora. Their signature of tucking a matchstick into the band of each hat perfectly embodies the free spirit and masculinity of their creations.

New Era

Easily the most recognizable of the brands listed here, New Era specializes in creating the best baseball caps in the game. With an exclusive with the MLB, these hats are on the cutting-edge of design, redefining the traditional team cap.
