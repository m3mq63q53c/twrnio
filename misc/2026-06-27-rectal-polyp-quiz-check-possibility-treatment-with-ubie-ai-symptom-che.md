What is Rectal Polyp?

This refers to small growths at the end of the large intestine found during colonoscopy. Most are harmless, but some can potentially turn into cancer over time.

Treatment of Rectal Polyp

Polyps are often removed on-the-spot during the colonoscopy depending on the size.
