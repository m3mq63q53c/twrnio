Are there any common side effects associated with berberine supplements?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Berberine supplements may cause side effects, most commonly gastrointestinal issues such as stomach upset, diarrhea, constipation, gas, and cramping. Less frequently, berberine can lead to headaches, low blood pressure, or interact with medications like blood thinners and diabetes drugs. Side effects are often dose-dependent, so lower doses may reduce discomfort.
