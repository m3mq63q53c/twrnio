Sleep apnea in children is often marked by loud snoring, pauses in breathing during sleep, restless sleep, mouth breathing, and daytime symptoms such as excessive sleepiness, difficulty concentrating, hyperactivity, or behavioral problems at school.

Because these signs can overlap with other conditions like allergies, ADHD, or enlarged tonsils, identifying the true cause matters.
