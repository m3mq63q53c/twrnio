# Style Road Rules by Christopher Bell

Speed and style have more in common than you think. Both demand precision, confidence, and the ability to make split-second decisions that turn heads for all the right reasons. For NASCAR Cup Series driver Christopher Bell, what you wear off the track says as much about your mindset as how you handle a turn at 200 miles per hour. Dressing well, like driving well, is about knowing what works, what doesn’t, and how to cross the finish line looking composed. It’s about being in complete control.

COOFANDY fits right into this lane. The global menswear brand, celebrating its 10th anniversary this year, has built its name on helping men “Dress the Journey,” crafting wardrobe staples that boost confidence from the boardroom to the starting line. To mark the milestone, the company has teamed up with Joe Gibbs Racing (JGR) and Bell for its first-ever motorsports partnership. The collaboration, built around the “Dress the Win” campaign, is about celebrating victories big and small—whether it’s a podium finish or simply nailing your morning routine.

Bell’s style philosophy runs on three green flags: versatility, timelessness, and quality. He looks for pieces that can shift gears from casual to refined, that won’t go out of style next season, and that feel as good as they look. COOFANDY checks every box. The brand’s men’s knit quarter zip, for instance, moves easily from pit lane to dinner plans, while the black-and-white flannel shirt is the kind of everyday essential that quietly pulls its weight in any lineup. These are pieces that are built for performance, and designed for longevity.

The Inside Track on Martinsville

Christopher Bell, driver of the No. 20 Toyota for Joe Gibbs Racing, has been firing on all cylinders in 2025. With 13 career Cup Series wins, including his fourth victory of the season at the Bristol Night Race and a $1 million payday at the All-Star Race, Bell has proven speed and precision aren’t just for the track—they’re a lifestyle. From three consecutive wins earlier this year to his first Daytona triumph in 2021, Bell’s career is a masterclass in consistency, ambition, and chasing victory at every turn.

He brought that same drive to the epic showdown at Martinsville. While, here Bell and COOFANDY showed off their style, both on and off the track. A style pitstop was created for fans to stop by and check out the COOFANDY wrapped JGR race car, get COOFANDY swag and there were plenty of photo opportunities. The VIP experience and meet and greet with Bell and head coach was also part of the 10 year anniversary celebration. The COOFANDY cheerleaders, COOFANDY Victory Angels posed with fans and cheered Christopher Bell on from the grandstands.
