How does the prevalence of ankle pain vary with age?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Ankle pain is a common issue that can affect people of all ages, though it most frequently occurs in young athletes and middle-aged adults. The causes vary widely: younger, active individuals often develop ankle pain from sports-related injuries such as sprains, strains, or fractures, while middle-aged and older adults may experience it due to chronic conditions like arthritis, tendonitis, or degenerative joint changes.

Explanation

Ankle pain is a common issue that can affect people differently depending on their age. Young Athletes: For young athletes, ankle pain is often due to sports activities. They might twist or sprain their ankles while playing, leading to persistent pain. Middle-Aged Adults: As people reach middle age, ankle pain can become more common due to chronic conditions. This might include arthritis or long-term wear and tear on the joints. These conditions can cause ongoing discomfort that needs careful management.

In summary, while young athletes may experience ankle pain from injuries, middle-aged individuals are more likely to face it due to chronic issues.
